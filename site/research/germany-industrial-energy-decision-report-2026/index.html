Executive preview · On-page extract

Four decisions begin with one distinction.

Wholesale electricity prices describe a traded market. Plant electricity bills also reflect grid fees, levies, taxes, procurement terms and site-specific operations. Analysis must keep these layers separate.

01 / Market

Characterise the price distribution.

For 2025, the independently checked DE–LU day-ahead price series averages approximately €89.32/MWh. The wholesale distribution contains both negative-price episodes and high-price intervals. That average is not a manufacturer's delivered tariff.

02 / Delivered cost

Compare identical tariff bands.

The report reviews Eurostat's H1 2026 industrial electricity bands IC–IG for Germany, France and Spain using the same tax treatment and currency. German observations in the extracted comparison carry provisional flags.

03 / Plant exposure

Decompose the plant's actual bill.

Consumption profile, contractual structure and grid-related charges influence exposure. The report proposes a decision framework to identify data needed before estimating company-specific savings.

04 / Capital allocation

Stress-test investment assumptions.

The illustrative ten-year battery-storage cash-flow model yields approximately €69,938 base-case NPV under its stated assumptions. Benefit reductions of 20% and 30% result in negative NPVs, demonstrating sensitivity rather than a deployable site recommendation.

Source context: Bundesnetzagentur / SMARD and Eurostat nrg_pc_205. Independently reproduced arithmetic is recorded in the research verification pipeline. Publication rights and final claim-level assurance are still under review.

Source and methodological transparency

Evidence you can examine.Assumptions you can challenge.

Wholesale market
SMARD / Bundesnetzagentur: 2025 market review. The source reports 573 negative-price hours. A separate interval-to-hour reconstruction counts 576 negative hourly means. Definitions remain under reconciliation.
Industrial tariffs
Eurostat, nrg_pc_205, H1 2026, DE / FR / ES, defined consumption bands and exclusion of VAT and recoverable taxes. Some German source cells are provisional.
Investment model
A deterministic illustrative ten-year discounted cash-flow analysis. Assumptions are disclosed above; site feasibility, dispatch scheduling and contract economics require separate project-level diligence.
Limitations
Data and policy statements have specific source dates. This preview is editorial work in progress. It provides no company-specific energy audit, investment advice or assured savings. Forecasting is separately gated and not part of this report.
