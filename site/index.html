Source transparency

Methodology & Transparency

Record each series’ source, release date, units and revisions. Document transformations and assumptions so a reader can reproduce the result.

Calibrate models against documented historical observations and check performance on periods outside the calibration window. The simulator uses a synthetic index to demonstrate the controls.

How to read data labels

Official historical
Published observations from the responsible statistical authority.
Provisional
Early source releases that may be revised.
Estimated
Values inferred using a documented method where observations are unavailable.
Forecast
Forward-looking values conditional on stated assumptions.
